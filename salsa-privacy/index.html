Salsa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Garden Games ("we", "us") makes the Salsa card game for Android and iOS ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your device: player name, avatar, coins, gems, level, statistics, settings, daily reward and mini game status, and Salsa Pass progress. We do not send this data to our own servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example to get an extra wheel spin). Ads are served by Google AdMob and by ad networks that work with it, such as Unity Ads. These partners may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app settings. On iOS, the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Unity privacy policy.

3. Purchases

Coin packs, gem packs, Premium and Salsa Pass are bought through the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. Payment details are handled by Google or Apple and never reach us. We only receive confirmation that a purchase is valid.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device, for example when your daily reward is ready. These are local notifications and do not use a server. You can turn them off in the app settings or in your phone settings.

5. Sharing

If you use the share button, your phone's own share menu opens. We do not see who you share with.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your choices

You can withdraw ad consent in the app settings, reset your advertising identifier in your phone settings, turn off notifications, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when new features are added. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
