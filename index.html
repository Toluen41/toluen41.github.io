Frequently Asked Questions

My purchase did not go through. What should I do?
Restore purchases from the settings screen inside the app. If the problem continues, email us with your purchase receipt.

How do I remove ads?
Ad removal is available as an in-app purchase in the shop section of the game.

The app crashes or does not open.
Make sure you are on the latest version from the App Store, then restart your device. If it persists, email us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Send us an email. We read every message.
