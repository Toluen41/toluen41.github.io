Common questions

My purchase did not go through
Open the settings screen inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. If nothing comes back, email us with the receipt you got from Apple or Google.
How do I remove ads?
Ad removal is a one time purchase in the shop section of each game.
The game crashes or will not open
Update to the latest version, then restart your device. If it keeps happening, email us with your device model and system version so we can reproduce it.
I found a bug, or I have an idea
Send it to the address above. Every message is read by the person who made the game.
